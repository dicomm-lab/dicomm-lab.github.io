Research

Secure Distributed Artificial Intelligence Network for AIoT

We study secure communication and distributed artificial intelligence for AIoT systems, with emphasis on wireless channels, low-power devices, edge intelligence, and security-aware system design.

Secure Communications for Unmanned Vehicles

We investigate secure and reliable communication mechanisms for unmanned vehicles operating in dynamic wireless environments, including UAV and autonomous network scenarios.

Hybrid Optimization

We develop hybrid methodologies that integrate machine learning, communication signal processing, and optimization for intelligent physical-layer system design.
